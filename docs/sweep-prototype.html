Things to try

The panel behaves like the native app. Everything resets when you reload.

  • Scan and clean

    Press the big Scan bubble, switch off anything you want to keep, then Clean. Only safe, rebuildable data is included.

  • Tap Pro

    See every rule and file, items that need review, app support files, leftovers from deleted apps, settings and undo history.

  • Change profiles

    In Pro › Settings, add Gaming or drop Development and scan again. The table on the right follows along.

  • Undo

    Anything that went to the Trash can be put back from the finished screen.
