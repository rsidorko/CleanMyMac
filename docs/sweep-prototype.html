Things to try

The panel behaves like the native app. Everything resets when you reload.

  • Review the scan

    Expand a rule to see each folder. Items an open app is using, or that are marked Review, start unchecked.

  • Switch profiles

    In the Profile tab, add Gaming or remove Development, then scan again. The table on the right updates as you go.

  • Clean, then undo

    Rebuildable caches are deleted outright; everything else goes to the Trash and can be put back.

  • Remove an unused app

    The Apps tab lists apps not opened in months, with their support files, plus data left behind by apps you already deleted.
